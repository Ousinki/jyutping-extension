LearnDuck 粵拼打字入門

Lesson 2 學校 School

學習目標 Learning Objectives

  1. 用粵拼打出和學校有關的詞語。
    Learn to type characters and words related to school in Jyutping.
  2. 學習韻母 e、ai 和 ik 的組合和常見詞語。
    Learn common characters and words with the rhymes e, ai, and ik.

重點詞語 Key Vocabulary

語音簡介 Phonetics Overview

簡介 Introduction


今次我們會學習另外 3 個韻母：e、ai、ik。
We will learn 3 rhymes: e, ai, ik.

注意事項 Points to Note


aai 和 ai 的分別在於韻腹的長度：aai 韻腹較長有兩個 a，ai 韻腹較短只有一個 a。
Here is a quick test to differentiate aai and ai. Try dragging out the vowels slowly: when you read aai, you will prolong the long “aa”; while when reading ai, you will arrive at the “i” much quicker and prolong the “i” instead (and not the short “a”).
老師可以用「街 gaai」和「雞 gai」、「大 daai」和「弟 dai」作例子，與學生一同分辨長短音。

聲旁介紹 Phonetic Components Overview

米mai
我們可以從字的聲旁大概猜到它的讀音。這些聲旁為「米」的字，讀音都是 mai。
From the phonetic component of a character, we can infer its approximate pronunciation. The following characters with the phonetic component「米」are pronounced mai.
迷
咪
謎
瞇

字的部首有意義分類的作用。部首為「言」的字，意思多與説話有關。
The radical of a character carries meaning and serves as a classification function. When the radical is ‘言’ (speech), the meaning of the character is related to speaking or words.
